#Passage: The water cycle, also known as the hydrologic cycle, describes how water evaporates from the surface of the earth, rises into the atmosphere, cools and condenses into rain or snow in clouds, and falls again to the surface as precipitation. The water that falls on land collects in rivers, lakes and underground aquifers, and much of it eventually flows back to the oceans, where the cycle begins again. The sun is the main driver of the water cycle because its heat causes evaporation. Without the water cycle, fresh water would not be replaced and life on land could not survive.

#Passage: The Solar System consists of the Sun and everything that orbits around it, including eight planets, their moons, dwarf planets, asteroids and comets. The planets in order from the Sun are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune. The four planets closest to the Sun are small and rocky, while the four outer planets are much larger and mostly made of gas. Asteroids are rocky objects that orbit the Sun, mostly between the orbits of Mars and Jupiter in a region called the asteroid belt. Comets are made of ice and dust and grow long glowing tails when they come close to the Sun.
